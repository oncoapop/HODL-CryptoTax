## 2024-11-05 - Empty States with Call-to-Action for DataFrames
**Learning:** Blank DataFrames in Streamlit can be confusing to users; checking `df.empty` and replacing them with a descriptive `st.info` message provides much-needed guidance.
**Action:** When rendering tabular data in Streamlit, always verify if the data is empty and provide a clear call-to-action instead of a blank container.
